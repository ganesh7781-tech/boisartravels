About Boisar Travels

Leading the way in premium travel solutions since 2018.

Your Trusted Partner in Every Journey

Boisar Travels was founded in 2018 with a simple yet powerful mission: to provide the most reliable, comfortable, and professional travel services in the Palghar district.

Over the years, we have grown from a small local service to a premier travel partner serving thousands of customers across Maharashtra. Our success is built on a foundation of punctuality, safety, and a customer-first approach.
