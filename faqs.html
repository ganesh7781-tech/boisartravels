Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book a cab with Boisar Travels?

You can book a cab instantly via WhatsApp by clicking any "Book Now" button on our website. Alternatively, you can call us directly at +91 86248 49674 for immediate assistance.

Do you provide 24/7 service?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We recommend booking at least 2 hours in advance for local trips and 12 hours for outstation trips to ensure availability.

Are your prices inclusive of Toll and Parking?

Our standard quotes for outstation trips usually exclude Toll, Parking, and State Taxes. For Airport Transfers, we provide all-inclusive transparent pricing. Always confirm the final breakdown with our executive during booking.

What types of vehicles do you offer?

We have a diverse fleet including Sedans (Dzire, Etios), SUVs (Ertiga, Innova Crysta), Luxury cars (Fortuner, Mercedes), and Group travel vehicles like Tempo Travellers and Buses.

Is it safe for women to travel late at night?

Safety is our top priority. All our drivers are locally verified and highly professional. We also share driver details and live location tracking for all night-time and outstation trips.
